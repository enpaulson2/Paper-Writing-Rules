# NRF 2027 Proposal Writing Rules and Readiness Tracker

**Target call:** 2027 MSIT Individual Basic Research Programme, First New-Project Call  
**Target scheme:** Core Research Grants - Type A  
**Working purpose:** Maintain the requirements, reviewer-facing criteria, evidence, and actions that the proposal should satisfy. Each entry is captured from the official guideline before proposal drafting begins.

## How to use this tracker

For every relevant page of the official guideline, record:

1. the source page and rule;
2. the practical implication for the Core Research Type A application;
3. the proposal section, evidence, or administrative action affected;
4. the status of the required action.

## Proposal readiness checklist

- [ ] Confirm all IRIS researcher-profile and institutional-registration requirements.
- [ ] Complete Type A PI submission and institutional-review steps ahead of the official deadlines.
- [ ] Verify the NRI record: education, career, Hanyang department, and five-year project history.
- [ ] Identify the exact Core Research Type A eligibility, duration, and funding rules.
- [ ] Extract the evaluation criteria, weights, and reviewer guidance.
- [ ] Map every criterion to a proposal section, evidence source, or planned figure/table.
- [ ] Verify all required forms, consents, and institutional approvals.
- [ ] Conduct a final compliance cross-check before IRIS submission.
